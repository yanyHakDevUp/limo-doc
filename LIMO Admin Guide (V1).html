LIMO Ride Admin Panel

Admin Staff Guide

This guide covers everything staff need to manage bookings, assign drivers, configure pricing, and keep the system running day-to-day. The admin panel is built on Filament and accessible at:

limoridekh.com/admin

All changes take effect immediately. There is no staging environment — act carefully when editing routes, fares, or cancelling bookings.

Logging In

1
Go to limoridekh.com/admin in a web browser.
2
Enter your email address and password provided by the system administrator.
3
You are taken to the Bookings list — the main working view.
Note: Only accounts created by the system administrator can log in. Contact the administrator to add new staff accounts or reset a password.

Booking List

The booking list is the main daily workspace. It shows all bookings sorted by newest first.

Filtering & searching

1
Use the Search box at the top to find bookings by customer name, phone, or booking number (e.g. LM-20251024-ABCD).
2
Click Filters to filter by status, route, or pickup date range.
3
Click any row to open the full booking detail.

Status Reference

Every booking moves through a series of statuses. Each status has a colour badge in the list view.

Awaiting Payment Booking created. Customer has received a payment link by SMS. Waiting for them to pay via KHQR and upload their slip.
Slip Submitted Customer uploaded a payment slip. Staff must review the slip and either verify or reject it.
Needs Attention Staff rejected the slip (unclear image, wrong amount, etc.). Customer was notified by SMS to re-upload.
Pending Driver Payment verified. Staff are assigning a driver and vehicle. Customer was notified their payment is confirmed.
Confirmed Driver assigned. Customer received a confirmation SMS with driver details, vehicle plate, and ticket link.
Completed Trip finished. Staff marked the booking as done after the ride.
Cancelled Booking cancelled — either by staff, by the customer, or automatically because payment was not submitted before the deadline.

Status Workflow

Below is the typical path a booking follows from creation to completion, and the actions staff take at each step.

Step 1 — Review a submitted slip

When a booking shows Slip Submitted, open it and scroll to the Payment Slip section to view the image the customer uploaded.

1
Click the booking to open the detail page.
2
Check the slip: correct amount in USD, correct account, legible image.
3
If OK — click Verify Payment. Status moves to Pending Driver. Customer is notified via their preferred channel (Telegram / SMS / WhatsApp).
4
If not OK — click Reject Slip, enter a short reason (e.g. "Amount incorrect, please re-upload"). Status moves to Needs Attention. Customer is notified via their preferred channel.

Step 2 — Assign a driver and confirm

Once payment is verified, see Assign Driver below.

Step 3 — Mark as completed

1
After the trip has taken place, open the booking.
2
Click Mark as Completed. Status moves to Completed. A thank-you message is sent to the customer via their preferred channel.

Cancelling a booking

1
Open the booking and click Cancel Booking.
2
Enter a cancellation reason. The customer will receive this in their SMS notification.
Note: Bookings that stay in Awaiting Payment or Needs Attention past their payment deadline are cancelled automatically by the system overnight. The customer is notified by SMS.

Assign Driver

Assigning a driver moves the booking to Confirmed and sends a confirmation message to the customer (via their preferred channel — Telegram, SMS, or WhatsApp) and a trip briefing SMS to the driver.

1
Open the booking (status must be Pending Driver).
2
Click Assign Driver & Confirm.
3
Select a Driver from the dropdown, or fill in the driver details manually (name, phone, vehicle model, plate number).
4
Click Confirm. The booking status changes to Confirmed and both SMSes are sent automatically.

What the customer receives

Your LIMO Ride trip (LM-20251024-ABCD) to Siem Reap is CONFIRMED! Driver: Sokha Meng (097 111 2222). Car: Mazda EZ-60 [PP 2B-8899]. Ticket: [link]

What the driver receives

LIMO Ride trip LM-20251024-ABCD. Pickup: 25 Oct 2025 08:00, Vattanac Tower. To: Siem Reap, Pub Street. Passenger: Chea Borey 012 345 678, 2 pax, 1 bag. Car: Mazda EZ-60 PP-2B-8899

Resend Notification

If a customer says they did not receive their confirmation or ticket, you can resend it from the booking detail page. The message is sent via whichever channel the customer chose when they booked (Telegram, SMS, or WhatsApp).

1
Open the confirmed booking.
2
Click Resend Ticket SMS in the action bar. The ticket link is sent again to the customer via their preferred channel.
Note: The Resend button only appears on bookings with status Confirmed or Completed.

Scan Ticket QR

At pickup, staff can scan a customer's QR ticket to instantly see their booking details without searching manually.

1
In the admin panel left menu, go to Operations → Scan Ticket.
2
Allow camera access when prompted. Point the camera at the QR code on the customer's ticket (printed or on their phone screen).
3
The booking details appear instantly — name, route, pickup time, driver, status. A link to the full booking edit page is also shown.

Manual fallback

If the camera is unavailable or QR scanning fails, type the Booking Number (e.g. LM-20251024-ABCD) into the search field on the Scan Ticket page.
Camera requires HTTPS: The camera will not work if the site is accessed over plain HTTP. If scanning is not working, check that the URL starts with https://. Use the manual fallback in the meantime.

Vehicle Classes

Vehicle Classes define the car types customers can choose when booking. Pricing per class is set separately in Routes & Fares.

Add a vehicle class

1
Go to Fleet Management → Vehicle Classes.
2
Click New Vehicle Class in the top-right corner.
3
Fill in the fields below and click Save.
Name Displayed to customers. e.g. Economy, Business, VIP, VAN Required
Max Passengers Maximum number of passengers this class can carry. e.g. 4, 6, 8 Required
Description Short note shown to customers on the booking form. e.g. "Comfortable sedan for up to 4 passengers" Optional
Photo Representative car image shown to customers Optional
Important: After adding a class, you must set a price for it in Routes & Fares for each route. Without a price, the class will not appear as bookable on that route.

Driver Management

Drivers are assigned to confirmed bookings. Their name, phone and vehicle plate are sent to the customer by SMS when a booking is confirmed.

Add a driver

1
Go to Fleet Management → Driver Management.
2
Click New Driver.
3
Fill in the fields and click Save. The driver will now appear in the dropdown when assigning to a booking.
Full Name Driver's name as it will appear in the customer SMS Required
Phone Driver's mobile number — also shown to the customer and used to send the trip briefing SMS Required
Vehicle Make and model. e.g. Toyota Camry, Mazda EZ-60 Required
Plate Number Vehicle registration plate. e.g. PP-1234A Required
Vehicle Class The class this driver serves (Economy, Business, VAN, etc.) Optional

Locations

Locations are the cities or places customers can select as their From / To point when booking. After adding a location you must create a Route connecting it to another location, or customers will see no service available.

Add a location

1
Go to Pricing & Routes → Locations.
2
Click New Location.
3
Enter the location name and click Save. Then go to Routes & Fares to connect it.
Name (English) Shown to customers. e.g. Phnom Penh, Siem Reap, Sihanoukville Required
Name (Khmer) Khmer spelling. e.g. ភ្នំពេញ Optional
Notes Internal notes — not shown to customers Optional

Routes & Fares

A route links two locations and carries the price for each vehicle class. Routes are directional — Phnom Penh → Siem Reap and Siem Reap → Phnom Penh are two separate routes with their own fares.

Create a route

1
Go to Pricing & Routes → Routes & Fares.
2
Click New Route. Select the From and To locations.
3
Set a base fare (USD) for each vehicle class. Leave a class blank or at zero to make it unavailable on this route.
4
Click Save. Customers can now book this route, and the prices will appear on the booking form.
Round trip pricing: The round-trip price is automatically calculated as one-way fare × 2. You only enter the one-way price.

Edit an existing route's fares

Click any route in the list and update the fare figures. Changes take effect immediately for new bookings. Existing bookings keep their original price.

Discounts

Discount codes let customers get a reduced fare. You share the code with them directly; they enter it during booking checkout.

Create a discount code

1
Go to Pricing & Routes → Discounts.
2
Click New Discount.
3
Fill in the fields and click Save. Share the code with customers.
Code What the customer types. Use uppercase, no spaces. e.g. WELCOME10, LIMO20 Required
Type Percentage (%) — reduces fare by a percentage. Fixed ($) — reduces fare by a flat dollar amount. Required
Value The discount amount. e.g. 10 means 10% off or $10 off depending on type. Required
Max Uses How many times this code can be used in total. Leave blank for unlimited. Optional
Expiry Date Date after which the code no longer works. Leave blank for no expiry. Optional
Route Restrict the code to one specific route. Leave blank to apply to all routes. Optional

Notification Channels

The system supports three channels for sending booking notifications to customers. Customers choose their preferred channel when they book.

ChannelStatusUsed for
Telegram Active Customer booking notifications (confirmation, slip rejected, completed). Also used for ops team internal alerts. Customers book via @limo_ride_bot.
SMS Configurable Customer notifications via PlasGate Cambodia SMS gateway. Requires a registered Sender ID. Currently in setup — see SMS Settings below.
WhatsApp Not yet active Planned for a future release. Will use the WhatsApp Business API with pre-approved message templates.
Currently: Telegram is the fully working customer notification channel. If a customer books via the website and does not use Telegram, they receive SMS (when the PlasGate Sender ID is approved). WhatsApp will be added in a future update.

SMS Settings

Configure the PlasGate SMS gateway for customers who choose SMS as their notification channel. Go to Settings → SMS & Customer Channels tab.

Enable SMS Toggle to enable or disable all automatic SMS sending. When off, no SMS is sent for any booking event.
SMS Provider PlasGate — Cambodia SMS gateway (live). Simulation — messages are logged to file only, nothing is sent to customers. Use Simulation for testing.
SMS Sender ID The name that appears as the sender on customer phones. Must be registered and approved by PlasGate. e.g. LimoRide (no spaces, max 11 characters).
PlasGate Private Key From your PlasGate dashboard — "Private" column. Paste the full key.
PlasGate Secret Key From your PlasGate dashboard — "Secret" column. Paste the full key.
Invalid Sender error: If you see "400 Invalid Sender" when sending, the Sender ID has not been registered in your PlasGate account. Log in to PlasGate, register the Sender ID, wait for approval (1–3 days), then update it here. Use Simulation mode in the meantime.

Test SMS

After entering your keys, use the Send Test SMS button on the Settings page to verify the gateway is working before going live. Enter your own phone number to receive the test message.

Telegram Settings

Telegram is the primary active notification channel. It notifies customers about their booking status and also alerts the operations team internally. Go to Settings → Telegram & Notifications tab.

Enable Telegram Toggle to enable or disable all Telegram notifications.
Bot Token The token from BotFather for the @limo_ride_bot Telegram bot. Format: 1234567890:AAB...
Ops Chat ID The Telegram chat ID of the operations group or channel that receives internal alerts.
Notify on Slip Upload When on, the ops group receives a Telegram message each time a customer submits a payment slip.
Notify on Driver Assigned When on, the ops group receives a Telegram message each time a driver is assigned to a booking.

Register Telegram Webhook

After updating the Bot Token, click Register Telegram Webhook (the green button on the Settings page). This connects the bot to the server so it can receive customer messages and commands. You need to do this once after each token change or server move.

Telegram Mini App: Customers can also book via the Telegram bot at t.me/limo_ride_bot. The Mini App is linked to the same booking system — bookings made via Telegram appear in the same admin booking list.
